What is driving the news flow

The primary driver is easing US-Iran friction and progress toward a ceasefire, which has reduced energy-driven inflationary pressures. This allows silver to recapture momentum during a high-stakes transition at the Federal Reserve. Diplomatic progress and the potential reopening of the Strait of Hormuz may alleviate macroeconomic imbalances, potentially allowing the Fed to pivot from aggressive hikes. Silver futures have rallied for four consecutive days, targeting the 200-day moving average. Focus has shifted from defensive hedging toward price discovery in a cooling inflation environment. While prices remain below yearly peaks, disciplined trading around technical swing levels suggests a strengthening recovery trend.

Supply & Mining

A structural deficit and misunderstood stockpile drawdowns provide a robust fundamental floor. This is reinforced by strong exploration results in districts like Argentina. These supply-side constraints suggest long-term value resilience regardless of short-term volatility in futures or geopolitical sentiment.

What to watch next

The June 19 peace deal signing will serve as a major indicator for regional stability and energy costs. Simultaneously, the first Fed rate decision under new leadership will determine if the rally sustains or fades under hawkish pressure. Key technical levels at 4,500 and 4,300, alongside global stockpile drawdowns, remain the primary metrics to monitor.
