Global Trade and Demand
Assesses global trade intensity and capital flows by combining USD regime signals, macro positioning (for example, supply stress), and speculative risk tone.

Regime Assessment:

  • Regime: Transitional / Fragile Balance
  • Regime Confidence Index: Medium Confidence
  • Status: Fragile

Why This Regime:

  • Supply stress and inflation themes remain the high-conviction cyclical drivers (1).
  • High energy hedger long shares (40.6%) indicate persistent physical market tightness (1).
  • Global risk-on sentiment is tactical and deteriorating toward the neutral threshold (2).
  • The USD Index is downweighted due to low conviction and mixed momentum signals (3).

Alignment & Tensions:

  • High-weight signals reinforce a narrative of supply-driven inflation in energy and agriculture (1).
  • A tension exists between the weakening tactical risk-on tone and stable cyclical themes (2).
  • USD price levels are bearish, yet momentum does not currently reinforce a regime shift (3).
  • Structural supply constraints prevent a shift to defensive labels despite slowing speculative breadth.

Scenario Balance:

  • Dominant scenario: Supply-driven inflationary pressure supported by commercial energy hedging (1).
  • Primary upside risk: Reversal of the positioning downtrend leading to renewed speculative expansion (2).
  • Primary downside risk: Mean reversion to a neutral or risk-off state if z-scores fail (2).

What Would Change the Regime:

  • A decline in energy hedger long positions below "deep long" thresholds (1).
  • The Global Risk-On z-score falling below the critical 0.50 level (2).
  • The USD Index price reclaiming its 12-month moving average of 120.08 (3).

USD Index Signal

Signal Summary:

  • Configuration statement: Given the USD Index price of 118.73 falling below its 12-month moving average of 120.08 while 6-month momentum remains at -2.18%, this setup aligns with Downward-biased price paths and Normal volatility, where the dominant risk is Regime shift, not trend continuation (1).
  • The signal has transitioned to a Bearish classification as of April 2026, following a brief one-month Bullish interruption (1).
  • Conviction Band: Low; Interpretation Confidence: Mixed Signals; Internal Conflict Flag: Yes. Signal Stability Assessment: Volatile; Threshold Proximity: Near; Revision Sensitivity: Unknown.

Methodology Applied:

  • Weakening regimes are defined by the index price staying below the 12-month moving average and a 6-month change below -5% (1).
  • Regime transitions or lack of conviction occur when there is a conflict between level (price vs. MA) and momentum thresholds (1).
  • Bullish signals imply tightening financial conditions, while Bearish signals suggest easing liquidity (1).
  • USD Index Signal: latest observation 2026-04-01 (1).

Key Dynamics:

  • The primary driver is the index price (118.73) crossing below the 12-month MA (120.08), triggering the Bearish label (1).
  • Internal Conflict: While the level is bearish, the 6-month momentum (-2.18%) has not yet reached the -5% threshold required for a "reinforcing" weakening regime (1).
  • Conditional Invalidation: A reversal of the price above the 12-month moving average (approx. 120.08) or a 6-month change exceeding +5% (1).

Scenario Balance:

  • Base case dominant: Continued USD softness as price remains below its trailing average, though lack of momentum suggests a slow easing process.
  • Upside risk: A sharp move above 120.08 triggered by narrowing yield differentials or a global risk-off impulse.
  • Downside risk: Acceleration of selling pressure pushing 6-month momentum below -5%, confirming a reinforced weakening regime.

Time Horizon & Aggregation:
