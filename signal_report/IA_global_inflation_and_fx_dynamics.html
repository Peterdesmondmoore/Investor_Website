Global Inflation and FX Dynamics
Interprets global inflation and FX conditions through US dollar regime and G10 currency positioning to characterise disinflationary pressures and cross-currency stress dynamics.

Regime Assessment:

  • Regime: Transitional / Fragile Balance
  • Regime Confidence Index: Medium Confidence
  • The regime is fragile as primary signals are currently testing major technical and positioning thresholds.

Why This Regime:

  • The regime is driven by a cyclical transition in the USD Index, which has moved below its 12-month moving average (1).
  • Tactical signals show a synchronized rotation out of the US Dollar, characterized by long reduction in USD (2) and aggressive long builds in CAD (3) and GBP (4).
  • Dynamic weighting prioritized the cyclical USD Index signal (1) and high-confidence G10 tactical flows (3)(5).
  • USD and YEN signals were downweighted due to internal conflicts and mixed interpretation confidence (2)(6).

Alignment & Tensions:

  • Alignment exists across G10 currencies (AUD, CAD, EUR, GBP, YEN) which all exhibit positive 4-week positioning flows (7)(3)(5)(4)(6).
  • Tension is evident in USD Spec Net %OI, which remains positive despite negative flow momentum, creating a "Bearish Reversal Risk" (2).
  • These tensions do not overturn the regime because the cyclical USD price trend has already broken key support (1).

Scenario Balance:

  • Dominant scenario: Persistent USD weakness as speculators continue de-risking from crowded long positions.
  • Primary upside risk: A sharp reversal in global risk appetite triggering defensive USD covering above its 12-month MA (1).
  • Primary downside risk: Accelerated JPY short-covering causing a disorderly unwind of global carry trades (6).

What Would Change the Regime:

  • USD Index price closing monthly above the 12-month moving average hysteresis band (1).
  • A reversal in CAD 4-week positioning flow to negative values, signaling an end to the G10 recovery (3).
