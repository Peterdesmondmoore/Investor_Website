Global Inflation and FX Dynamics
Interprets global inflation and FX conditions through US dollar regime and G10 currency positioning to characterise disinflationary pressures and cross-currency stress dynamics.

Regime Assessment:

  • Regime: Transitional / Fragile Balance
  • Regime Confidence Index: Medium Confidence
  • The regime is fragile.

Why This Regime:

  • The cyclical USD index remains in a bearish regime below its 12-month average (7).
  • High-weight tactical signals indicate a broad transition away from extreme G10 positioning (3)(1).
  • CAD and GBP have normalized into balanced or neutral market structures (2)(4).
  • The USD CoT signal was downweighted due to mixed signals and internal conflict (5).

Alignment & Tensions:

  • EUR and JPY signals reinforce each other via active short-covering and upward-biased paths (3)(6).
  • Tensions exist in the USD CoT signal where price increases diverge from declining speculative sponsorship (5).
  • These tensions do not overturn the regime as primary cyclical trends remain bearish (7).

Scenario Balance:

  • Dominant scenario: Price consolidation as G10 speculators and hedgers reach temporary equilibrium (1)(4).
  • Primary upside risk: Renewed USD strength if the 6-month change breaches +5% (7).
  • Primary downside risk: Accelerated USD liquidation triggered by systemic EUR or JPY short-squeezes (3)(6).

What Would Change the Regime:

  • A USD monthly close above the 12-month moving average of approximately 119.80 (7).
  • Speculator Z-scores in major pairs returning to extreme crowding thresholds above +/- 2.0 (1)(3).
