US Inflation and Price Dynamics
Systematically synthesises realised inflation, cost pressures, and market expectations to assess the prevailing US inflation regime.

Regime Assessment:

  • Regime: Transitional / Fragile Balance
  • Regime Confidence Index: Medium Confidence
  • Stability: Fragile

Why This Regime:

  • Rising headline PCE momentum (2) and a sharp acceleration in producer prices (5) are challenging current neutral states.
  • The primary drivers include strengthening 5-year inflation breakevens (3) and a shift in policy-relevant inflation toward the "Hot" threshold (2).
  • Dynamic weighting prioritizes signals with High Interpretation Confidence and Medium Aggregation Weights (2)(3)(4)(5) over lower-conviction data.
  • The US Inflation Signal (1) was downweighted due to internal conflict and low confidence. Wage growth (6) was downweighted due to its low conviction band.

Alignment & Tensions:

  • Realized pricing data reinforces market term-structure shifts, as both Headline PCE and 5-year breakevens are trending higher (2)(3).
  • Tension exists between surging producer prices (5) and anchored market expectations (4) and wage growth (6).
  • Tensions do not overturn the regime call because high-weight cyclical signals are approaching critical regime-shift thresholds (5)(3).

Scenario Balance:

  • Dominant Scenario: Fragile neutrality persists as anchored expectations offset pipeline price spikes.
  • Primary Upside Risk: A shift to a "Hot" regime if PPI acceleration forces a downstream PCE breach (2)(5).
  • Primary Downside Risk: A return to disinflationary "Cool" territory if demand deceleration offsets producer costs (1).

What Would Change the Regime:

  • A PCE Composite Score breach above the 0.75 threshold into "Hot" status (2).
  • An Inflation Term-Structure Composite score exceeding 0.75, triggering a transition to Reflation (3).
  • The Market Implied Inflation z-score exceeding 1.0, indicating unanchored expectations (4).
