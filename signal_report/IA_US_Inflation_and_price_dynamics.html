US Inflation and Price Dynamics
Systematically synthesises realised inflation, cost pressures, and market expectations to assess the prevailing US inflation regime.

Regime Assessment:

  • Regime: Transitional / Fragile Balance
  • Regime Confidence Index: Medium Confidence
  • Status: Fragile

Why This Regime:

  • The primary driver is the "transition state" of the latent inflation composite, where a near-zero z-score implies high regime-flip risk (1).
  • While current realized policy-relevant inflation remains neutral (2), high-weight market expectations (3) and term-structure dynamics (4) show strong momentum toward reflationary thresholds.
  • The Wage Growth signal was downweighted due to Low Interpretation Confidence and Low Conviction (5).

Alignment & Tensions:

  • Market-based signals align, with both 5-year breakevens (3) and the term-structure composite (4) strengthening toward positive regimes.
  • A major tension exists between the cooling latent macro composite (1) and the rapid acceleration in producer prices, which indicates pipeline pressure (6).
  • Tensions do not overturn the call because the majority of high-confidence signals are currently clustered near critical threshold boundaries, confirming a transitional state.

Scenario Balance:

  • Dominant scenario: Persistent neutrality as Core PCE offsets headline volatility (2).
  • Primary upside risk: Transition to a "Hot" regime triggered by PPI velocity leading a sharp downstream CPI acceleration (6).
  • Primary downside risk: A move into a "Cool" regime driven by systemic demand destruction and industrial production weakness (1).

What Would Change the Regime:

  • A PCE composite score breach above the 0.75 threshold, indicating an inflationary expansion (2).
  • A reversal in the latent inflation composite z-score to above +0.25, invalidating the current cooling trend (1).
