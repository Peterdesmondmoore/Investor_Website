US Inflation and Price Dynamics
Systematically synthesises realised inflation, cost pressures, and market expectations to assess the prevailing US inflation regime.

Regime Assessment:

  • Regime: Transitional / Fragile Balance
  • Regime Confidence Index: Medium Confidence
  • The regime is fragile.

Why This Regime:

  • The primary driver is a "HOT" regime shift in upstream producer prices (1).
  • This signal carries high aggregation weight and high interpretation confidence (1).
  • Realized policy inflation and market expectations are nearing expansionary thresholds (2)(3).
  • The "COOL" inflation signal was downweighted due to low confidence and internal conflict (4).
  • Wage growth was downweighted due to its low interpretation confidence and quarterly lag (5).

Alignment & Tensions:

  • Headline PCE and forward breakevens reinforce the current upward momentum (2)(6).
  • Tensions exist as wage growth remains anchored within historical norms (5).
  • Tensions do not overturn the regime because pipeline inflation often leads consumer price adjustments (1).

Scenario Balance:

  • Dominant scenario: Upstream price pressure gradually forces a broader inflationary regime shift.
  • Primary upside risk: Market-implied inflation z-scores exceeding 1.0, triggering a "RISING" regime (3).
  • Primary downside risk: A sharp contraction in industrial production cooling producer price velocity (4).

What Would Change the Regime:

  • A PCE composite score breach above 0.75 would shift the regime to expansionary (2).
  • A divergence z-score retrenchment below 0.35 would signal the end of pipeline pressure (1).
  • A reversal in 5-year breakevens pulling the term-structure composite below its moving average (6).
