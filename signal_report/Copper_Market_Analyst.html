Market Drivers: The prevailing upside bias is supported by several recurring themes: acute mine supply disruptions, widening refined copper deficits, and strategic stockpiling. With the International Copper Study Group and other industry bodies confirming a transition from surplus to deficit, the institutional distribution suggests that the primary risk to these targets remains on the upside, specifically regarding the timing of supply normalization and the impact of global trade tariffs.
