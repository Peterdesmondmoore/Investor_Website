US Liquidity and Monetary Conditions
Synthesises Federal Reserve liquidity measures, money supply, real-rate signals and SOFR positioning to assess the current US monetary and liquidity regime.

Regime Assessment:

  • Regime: Transitional / Fragile Balance.
  • Regime Confidence Index: Medium Confidence.
  • The regime is fragile.

Why This Regime:

  • The primary driver is a conflict between improving liquidity and restrictive real rates.
  • 10-year real yields (1.91%) remain significantly above the 6-month trend (3).
  • The Fed Liquidity Composite is improving but remains in a Neutral state (1).
  • M2 momentum is bullish, but low year-over-year growth creates an internal conflict (2).
  • SOFR 3M signals were downweighted due to Low Interpretation Confidence and tactical time horizons (4).

Alignment & Tensions:

  • Liquidity indicators reinforce each other through falling Reverse Repo usage and rising M2 momentum (1)(2).
  • Tensions exist between expansionary liquidity trends and restrictive real yield policy stances (3).
  • Front-end positioning shows extreme short crowding while 5-year positioning remains balanced (4)(5).
  • Tensions do not overturn the regime because cyclical drivers currently lack a unified directional impulse.

Scenario Balance:

  • Dominant scenario: Range-biased price action as liquidity stabilization offsets restrictive real rates.
  • Primary upside risk: Rapid depletion of Reverse Repo facilities triggering an Expansionary regime shift (1).
  • Primary downside risk: Real yields accelerating higher, deepening the restrictive impulse (3).

What Would Change the Regime:

  • The Liquidity Composite exceeding 0.75 would signal an Expansionary regime shift (1).
  • Real yields falling within 0.05% of the 6-month trend would invalidate the Bearish stance (3).
  • A drop in M2 3-month momentum below -1.0% would trigger a shift toward a Defensive regime (2).

  • Rising values in the composite indicate expansionary liquidity and inflationary support, while falling values indicate drainage (1).
